A Structured Approach

Energy procurement
shouldn't be a
black box.

International energy transactions can involve multiple parties, commercial considerations, product specifications, logistics requirements and documentation.

Meridian brings these moving parts into a structured procurement process so that the requirement can be assessed before the most appropriate sourcing route is pursued.

Every transaction is different. Our process is therefore designed to provide structure without forcing every requirement into the same model.

The Meridian Process

Six stages from requirement to execution.

Our process provides a clear framework for procurement while allowing the commercial and logistical details of each requirement to be handled individually.

01

Requirement

The process begins with a clear understanding of what the buyer requires. Product, quantity, destination, timeline, specification and other relevant requirements are established.

What we establish

Product • Volume • Destination • Timeline • Specification

02

Assessment

The requirement is reviewed to determine the appropriate procurement route. Market conditions, supply availability, logistics and commercial considerations may all influence the sourcing strategy.

What we assess

Market • Availability • Logistics • Commercial Requirements

03

Strategic Sourcing

Appropriate supplier and supply-side opportunities are identified through Meridian's procurement network and international market relationships.

What we source

Product • Supplier • Origin • Supply Route

04

Structuring & Verification

Commercial requirements, documentation and transaction considerations are coordinated between the relevant parties. Information is reviewed as part of the transaction process.

What we coordinate

Documentation • Commercial Terms • Counterparties • Requirements

05

Transaction Support

Once an appropriate supply opportunity has been identified, Meridian assists with communication and coordination between the relevant parties as the transaction progresses.

What we support

Communication • Documentation • Coordination • Progress

06

Execution & Delivery

The transaction moves into execution, with delivery and logistics requirements coordinated according to the agreed commercial structure and destination.

Final stage

Execution • Logistics • Delivery • Completion

Start With Clarity

The more precise the requirement, the better the procurement process.

A well-defined requirement allows Meridian to assess the available supply routes more effectively and identify the factors that could affect pricing, availability and delivery.

  • Product and grade required
  • Required quantity or volume
  • Delivery destination
  • Required delivery timeline
  • Technical or quality specifications
  • Commercial or logistical considerations

What We Need From You

Give us the information needed to move the requirement forward.

You do not need to know the entire procurement process before approaching Meridian. Start with the fundamentals and our team can help structure the requirement.

01

Product

Tell us which energy product you require, including grade or specification where applicable.

02

Volume

Provide the required quantity, estimated consumption or recurring volume where known.

03

Destination

Tell us where the product needs to be delivered or where the transaction is intended to take place.

04

Let us know when the product is required and whether the requirement is once-off or recurring.

05

Basic buyer information helps us understand the commercial context and route the requirement appropriately.

06

Include any specifications, logistics, documentation or other conditions relevant to the procurement requirement.
