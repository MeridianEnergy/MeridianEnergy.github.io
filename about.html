Who We Are

Connecting demand with opportunity.

Meridian Energy Management is a South African energy procurement and supply company focused on connecting businesses with established international energy markets and supply networks.

We operate across the energy value chain, helping clients identify suitable supply opportunities, coordinate procurement, support transactions and navigate the practical requirements associated with international energy supply.

Our approach combines global market access with local understanding — giving our clients a more structured way to source the energy products they need.

Our Approach

Global markets.
Local understanding.

Energy procurement can be complex. Different markets, suppliers, logistics requirements, documentation, pricing structures and transaction processes all need to align before a supply agreement can become a successful delivery.

Meridian exists to help simplify that process.

We work to understand the client's requirement first, then coordinate the sourcing, supplier engagement and transaction support required to move the opportunity forward.
