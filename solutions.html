Our Capability

More than sourcing.
A complete procurement approach.

Energy procurement is rarely as simple as finding a product and agreeing on a price. Supply availability, specification, volumes, documentation, logistics, commercial terms and counterparty requirements all need to align.

Meridian brings these elements together through a coordinated procurement approach designed around the individual requirements of each client.

Whether the requirement involves a recurring fuel supply, a large-volume transaction or a specific international sourcing requirement, our role is to help create a clearer path from demand to supply.
