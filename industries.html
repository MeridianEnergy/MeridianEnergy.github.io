What We Consider

Procurement begins with understanding the operation.

A commodity is only one part of a successful energy transaction. We look at the wider requirement before developing the procurement route.

01

Consumption

Current and projected fuel or energy consumption helps establish the appropriate procurement scale and frequency.

02

Specification

Product specifications and quality requirements are assessed before appropriate supply options are considered.

03

Destination

Delivery location can materially influence sourcing, logistics and the overall commercial structure.

04

Timeline

Urgent, spot and recurring requirements can require different procurement approaches.

05

Logistics

Transportation, storage and delivery considerations are factored into the broader supply requirement.

Commercial Structure

Transaction requirements and commercial expectations are considered when structuring the procurement route.
